Services

Diminished Value Appraisals

Our industry-recognized appraisal method will provide you with a comprehensive and detailed report on the market-related diminished value your vehicle has suffered as a result of an accident that was not your fault. Your report will break down each category in detail with a full explanation of the reason your vehicle has lost market value. This report will provide you with the tool (proof) you need to collect what is owed to you!

Third-Party Appraisals (Total Loss)

If your vehicle was deemed a total loss by the insurance company and you are not satisfied with their offer, you have the right to obtain an independent appraisal to determine the actual cash value of your vehicle. We will determine the fair actual cash value of your vehicle and will also negotiate the settlement in an arbitration case.

Classic Car Appraisals

Our bumper-to-bumper 90-point inspection process will evaluate the body, structure, frame, interior, electrical, and mechanical components of the classic car. We will then provide a complete valuation of your vehicle based on our nationwide research.

Appraisals for Titles

Some states require appraisals to obtain a lost title or for tax purposes. We will come to your location to inspect your vehicle.

Appraisals for Insurance Policy

If you wish to have a stated value policy, the insurance company will require an appraisal. We will come to your location to inspect your vehicle.

Lease Turn-In Inspections

We perform lease turn-in inspections for leasing companies. Contact us for more information on this service.

Estate Appraisals

For bankruptcy, divorces, or estate sales, Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers can provide an independent certified appraisal to help you establish the true market value of automobiles.

Loans and Pre-Purchase Inspections

Before you buy your next automobile, have an independent inspection completed by Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers. We will inspect the body and structural components as well as the interior. A full mechanical inspection may need to be done by a mechanic if we identify some mechanical problems. With Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers' professional inspection service, you can buy with confidence.
