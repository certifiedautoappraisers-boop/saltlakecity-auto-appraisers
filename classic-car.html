Expert Classic & Collector Car Appraisals

Classic, vintage, and collector cars occupy a unique market driven by rarity, provenance, originality, and condition — factors that standard vehicle valuation guides simply cannot capture. Whether you own a muscle car from the 1960s, a pre-war European roadster, a production-era race car, or a meticulously restored American classic, Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers has the expertise to document your vehicle's true value with precision.

Our ASCAA-certified appraisers bring 26+ years of experience to every classic car appraisal, combining a rigorous 90-point physical inspection with comprehensive nationwide market research to produce USPAP-compliant reports accepted by insurance companies, courts, the IRS, banks, and estate attorneys throughout Utah and Georgia.

Our 90-Point Bumper-to-Bumper Inspection

Every classic car appraisal by Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers includes a comprehensive 90-point inspection that leaves nothing to chance. We evaluate all of the following categories in detail:

  • Body & Paint: Panel alignment, paint quality, evidence of prior repairs, rust, and corrosion
  • Structure & Frame: Unibody or body-on-frame integrity, evidence of collision or flood damage
  • Interior: Upholstery, carpeting, headliner, dashboard, gauges, and trim originality
  • Electrical Systems: Lighting, wiring, instrumentation, and accessory function
  • Engine & Drivetrain: Engine bay cleanliness, numbers matching, performance indicators, transmission condition
  • Undercarriage: Frame rails, floor pans, suspension components, exhaust, and driveshaft

When You Need a Classic Car Appraisal

Stated-Value Insurance

Insurance companies require a certified appraisal to issue an agreed-value or stated-value policy on a classic vehicle. Our appraisals are accepted by all major collector car insurers.

Estate & Probate

Estate attorneys and probate courts require IRS-compliant vehicle appraisals. We provide date-of-death valuations and fair market value reports for all classic vehicles in an estate.

Pre-Purchase Inspection

Before spending five or six figures on a classic car, know exactly what you're buying. Our inspection identifies hidden problems and confirms the seller's representations about originality and condition.

Divorce & Legal Proceedings

Utah courts require certified vehicle appraisals for equitable distribution in divorce proceedings. Our reports are prepared to withstand scrutiny in depositions and court hearings.

Financing & Collateral

Banks and specialty lenders require certified appraisals for classic car loans and collateral lending. Our USPAP-compliant reports satisfy all major lending institutions.

Charitable Donation

The IRS requires a qualified appraisal by a certified appraiser for vehicle donations valued over $500. Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers provides IRS-compliant appraisals for tax deduction purposes.
