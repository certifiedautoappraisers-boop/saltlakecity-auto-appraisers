What Documents Should You Upload?

Depending on the type of appraisal you are requesting, please gather and upload the appropriate documents listed below. The more complete your submission, the faster we can process your appraisal.

📉

Diminished Value

Vehicle photos, repair estimates, repair receipts, and accident/insurance reports.

🚗

Classic Car Appraisal

Build sheets, window stickers, photos (interior & exterior), and any restoration records.

📋

Total Loss Claims

Insurance settlement offers, total loss letters, vehicle title, and photos of the vehicle.

📄

General Appraisal

Any supporting documents, photos, or records relevant to your vehicle's value.
