Probate & Inheritance Vehicle Appraisals

Utah probate proceedings require that all estate assets, including motor vehicles, be valued at fair market value as of the date of death. Under Utah Statute § 733.212 and related probate rules, an accurate, independent vehicle appraisal is essential for the personal representative to properly inventory estate assets and distribute them according to the decedent's wishes or Utah intestacy law.

For estates containing classic cars, collector vehicles, motorcycles, or high-value automobiles, a certified appraisal is even more critical — both to satisfy the probate court and to protect the interests of all beneficiaries. Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers provides date-of-death retroactive valuations as well as current fair market value appraisals, both accepted by Fulton and Davis County probate courts.

Our reports meet IRS requirements for estate tax purposes, are accepted by Utah probate courts, and provide heirs and personal representatives with the documentation needed to close estates efficiently.

Divorce & Equitable Distribution Appraisals

Utah is an equitable distribution state under Utah Statute § 61.075, requiring courts to distribute marital assets fairly between divorcing spouses. When marital assets include vehicles — whether everyday transportation, classic cars, motorcycles, or recreational vehicles — an independent, certified appraisal is required to establish fair market value for division purposes.

Without a certified appraisal, each spouse may claim different values, leading to disputes that delay proceedings and increase legal costs. Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers provides impartial, court-accepted vehicle appraisals that give attorneys and judges a defensible starting point for fair asset distribution. Our USPAP-compliant reports have been accepted in Fulton, DeKalb, and Utah County family courts throughout the Salt Lake City metro area.

Bankruptcy Appraisals

Bankruptcy trustees and courts require independent, certified appraisals. Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers provides court-accepted bankruptcy appraisals for debtors, trustees, and creditors throughout the Salt Lake City Division of the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Middle District of Georgia.

Charitable Donation Appraisals

The IRS requires a qualified appraisal by a certified appraiser for vehicle donations with a claimed deduction over $500 (IRS Form 8283). Only appraisals performed by a "qualified appraiser" as defined under IRC § 170 and Treasury Regulation § 1.170A-17 meet this standard. Our ASCAA certification qualifies us to provide IRS-compliant charitable donation appraisals.

Trust & Guardianship Appraisals
