Diminished Value & Total Loss Claims for Murray Drivers

Utah is one of the strongest states in the country for diminished value recovery. The Utah Supreme Court’s landmark ruling in Allstate v. Hawkins (2001) established that insurers are obligated to compensate Utah policyholders for the inherent diminished value of their vehicles after a covered loss — even under first-party collision coverage, and even after complete repairs. For third-party claims against the at-fault driver’s insurer, Utah law is even clearer: you are entitled to the full permanent reduction in your vehicle’s fair market value.

Insurance companies routinely undervalue diminished value and total loss claims in Murray and across Davis County. Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers arms you with a certified, USPAP-compliant appraisal report documenting your loss with the credibility required to compel a fair settlement — and, when necessary, to invoke the appraisal clause or prevail at trial. We’ve helped Murray-area vehicle owners recover thousands of dollars beyond the insurer’s initial offer. Free evaluation before you commit.
