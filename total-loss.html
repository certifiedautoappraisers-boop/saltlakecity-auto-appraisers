Your Car Was Totaled — Now What?

When an insurance company declares your vehicle a total loss, they make an offer based on their own valuation — an offer that is frequently lower than your vehicle's true fair market value. Insurance companies use automated software tools that routinely underestimate comparable vehicle values, overlook recent condition improvements, or apply inappropriate adjustments.

You are not required to accept the insurance company's first offer. Under Utah law and the appraisal provisions in most insurance policies, you have the right to challenge the amount with an independent, certified total loss appraisal. Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers determines the actual cash value of your vehicle and fights to get you what you deserve.

Utah Total Loss Law & Your Rights

Utah Administrative Code Rule 69O-166.0301 governs how insurers must calculate actual cash value for total loss vehicles. The rule requires insurers to identify and use comparable vehicles in the local or regional market, apply documented adjustments for mileage and condition, and provide a written explanation of their valuation methodology.

Despite these requirements, insurer valuations often fall short of true market value. Utah Statute § 626.9641 also protects policyholders against unfair claim settlement practices. If your insurer uses biased software, refuses to consider all comparables, or fails to explain their methodology, Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers can provide the independent appraisal and documentation needed to compel a fair settlement — through negotiation, the appraisal clause arbitration process, or litigation support.
