Diminished Value & Total Loss Claims for West Jordan Drivers

Utah is one of the strongest states in the country for diminished value recovery. The Utah Supreme Court’s landmark ruling in Allstate v. Hawkins (2001) established that insurers are obligated to compensate Utah policyholders for the inherent diminished value of their vehicles after a covered loss — even under first-party collision coverage, and even after complete repairs. For third-party claims against the at-fault driver’s insurer, Utah law is even clearer: you are entitled to the full permanent reduction in your vehicle’s fair market value.

Insurance companies routinely undervalue diminished value and total loss claims in West Jordan and across North Salt Lake County. Utah Diminished Value Claims — West Jordan Drivers

West Jordan and North Salt Lake County drivers involved in accidents that were not their fault have the right to file a third-party diminished value claim against the at-fault driver's insurance carrier under Utah tort law. While Utah is a tort state, meaning at-fault drivers are responsible for both bodily injury and property damage, property damage — including permanent loss of vehicle market value — is handled separately and is fully recoverable.

A certified diminished value appraisal from Salt Lake City Auto Appraisers documents exactly how much your vehicle's resale value has decreased as a result of the accident history. Our reports are accepted by all Utah insurers and courts, including the Sixth Judicial Circuit covering Davis County. Utah's four-year statute of limitations gives you time, but acting sooner produces better results — repair records are fresher and comparables are more accurate.
